GamsGo Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

What the app does

GamsGo Uploader is an internal tool run on the channel owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos and set their metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, privacy and scheduling) on the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

The app requests the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. It reads only the authorized channel's ID, title and handle (to confirm the correct channel) and the videos it uploads. It does not access any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Tokens can be deleted at any time, and access can be revoked at Google Account permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

By using this app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
