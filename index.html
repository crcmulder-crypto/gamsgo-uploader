GamsGo Uploader

GamsGo Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the "GamsGo Guide" YouTube channels (Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian, English and Japanese editions). It uploads the owner's own finished videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and caption files to those channels through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public and no other users' accounts are accessed.
